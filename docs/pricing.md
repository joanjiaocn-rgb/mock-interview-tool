# Pricing Calibration

## Current v0
Free. Manual practice, timer, scoring, and local text export.

## Pro Concept
Not launched. Possible paid value:
- AI follow-up questions.
- Saved practice history.
- Resume-aware prompt generation.
- Transcript and evidence rewrite suggestions.
- Interview coach templates.

## Cost Notes
Pricing cannot be finalized until AI provider cost, storage policy, expected usage, and abuse limits are confirmed.

## Entitlement Draft
- Free: unlimited local browser sessions, no account, no AI.
- Pro: fixed monthly AI scoring credits, saved session history, export templates.
- Team: waitlist/contact only until B2B admin scope is defined.
